About

A small studio with a big standard

Kacrylx is owned by Kymani, a nail artist who built the studio around the experience she wanted as a client: a clean space, unhurried appointments and work that holds up.

Two artists work here. Between them they cover everything from a simple gel manicure to detailed art, line work and bling sets — plus men's manicures and pedicures.

Appointments are one at a time, so the studio stays calm and every set gets full attention.
